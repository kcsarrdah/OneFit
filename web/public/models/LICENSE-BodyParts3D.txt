Modifications (scripts/build-body-model.mjs):
- Kept bone and muscle meshes; dropped ligaments, joints and tendons.
- Grouped muscles into OneFit muscle groups, one mesh per group and side.
- Added generated latissimus dorsi and rectus abdominis meshes (not present in
  BodyParts3D), shaped over the source anatomy by scripts/lib/synthesize.mjs.
- Replaced the skull with a generated, stylised mannequin head sized from it.
- Added a generated subcutaneous fat envelope (scripts/lib/fatLayer.mjs) built
  around the source muscles and bones.
- Added a skeleton rig (scripts/lib/rig.mjs) with skin weights for posing.
- Recentred, simplified (meshoptimizer), quantized and meshopt-compressed.
